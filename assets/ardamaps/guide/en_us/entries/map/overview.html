This guide comes with a map it is accessed by right clicking while holding this guide in your hands or pressing the key. By default exploration is active, meaning that during your travels in Middle-earth, you will uncover more of the Map.
Clicking the coordinates button at the bottom of the screen will centre the map on your position.


Right clicking on the map allows you to:

  • Set a waypoint that will be displayed on the compass.
  • Teleport to that location if the area is Visited.
