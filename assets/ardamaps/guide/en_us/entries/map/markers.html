Interesting locations and points of interests are displayed and can be filtered using the Marker Selection menu on the center top of the map screen



The locations are interactive - markers can be clicked - and displayed in three categories :


  • Visible: indicates that there is a point of interest but its type (House, Fortification, etc) has not been discovered yet.
  • Revealed : indicates that the type of location is known.
  • Visited : means that you visited the place. Additional information is available when clicking on said marker on the map. You can teleport to any visited location using the button on the side panel.
