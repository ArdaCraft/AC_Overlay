It is a tool that displays the points of interests you can see in the landscape around you.
Only the explored locations will be shown on the Toposcope.

The Toposcope is accessed either by holding this guide in your hands or pressing the key.

When looking at a point of interest, its name (if explored) and distance is shown.

  • If a location is explored a waypoint can be set with a right click.
  • If a location is visited you can teleport to it with a left click.

Configuration


The Toposcope can be configured in the Options menu of this guide. You can change its opacity and the maximum distance at which points of interest are shown.
